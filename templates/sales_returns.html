Return rules

  • Resellable goods return to available stock. Damaged and unusable goods do not.
  • Refunds are capped at recorded customer receipts; returned value is prorated from the original sale after discount.
  • Debt adjustments require a registered customer with enough outstanding balance.
  • Replacement issues the same product and quantity. If returned goods are damaged, available stock is required.
