External data-sharing notice

If enabled, a user's explicit “Ask with AI” action sends the selected date period, an allow-listed topic, currency, and application-computed aggregate sales/stock metrics to the configured external provider. Owners' requests may also include aggregate profit, expense, and combined customer/supplier balance totals. Attendant requests exclude those owner-only financial fields and use only that attendant's sales totals plus shared stock counts.

No business or person names, contact details, account-level records, product names, transaction rows, IDs, credentials, or the user's free-text question are sent. The app computes and displays all figures; AI output is narrative-only, and responses containing numbers are discarded. The provider may process submitted aggregates under its own terms and may charge usage fees.

AI is disabled by default. Local, non-AI insights and reports continue to work if AI is disabled or unavailable. AI explanations may be inaccurate and are not accounting advice.
